Witness Statement - J. Marsh
Date of incident: 14 October 2026

I saw an unfamiliar white van parked outside the warehouse on Draymond
Street around 23:40. The driver was on the phone and seemed to be
reading something off a laptop screen. I did not get a clear look at
the plate but I believe it started with LK19.

The password mentioned on the phone call sounded like "falcon2024" but
I cannot be certain. He left in a hurry around 23:55 when a security
light activated.
